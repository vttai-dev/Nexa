NEXA.PLATFORM - CHUC NANG DA CO
Cap nhat: 2026-09-28

1. Kien truc du an
- Solution .NET 10 theo Clean Architecture.
- Cac project backend:
  - Nexa.Domain: entity va rule nghiep vu.
  - Nexa.Application: use case, handler, contract, abstraction.
  - Nexa.Identity: user, role, cookie auth, ASP.NET Core Identity.
  - Nexa.Infrastructure: EF Core, PostgreSQL, repository, migration, seed data.
  - Nexa.Api: Minimal API, DI composition root, health check, OpenAPI.
- Frontend Next.js nam trong thu muc nexa-web, da co trang cong dong, bai viet, dashboard tac gia va admin co ban.

2. Database va ha tang
- PostgreSQL lam database chinh.
- EF Core DbContext: NexaDbContext.
- Migration dau tien: InitialCreate.
- Docker Compose cho PostgreSQL local.
- Script khoi tao moi truong development: scripts/Initialize-Development.ps1.
- Health checks:
  - /health/live
  - /health/ready
- Seed role mac dinh:
  - Member
  - Moderator
  - Admin
- Co lenh migrate/seed/bootstrap admin qua CLI.

3. Bao mat nen tang
- Dang nhap bang cookie first-party.
- Cookie session dung prefix __Host-Nexa.Session.
- CSRF protection cho cac request ghi du lieu.
- Endpoint lay CSRF token:
  - GET /api/v1/auth/csrf
- Rate limit cho dang ky/dang nhap.
- HTTPS local profile.
- Khong dung JWT o giai doan nay.
- Dang ky can email lam tai khoan; so dien thoai tuy chon. Khong chan dang nhap khi chua co provider.
- Email/SMS verification va reset token da co ma nguon nhung mac dinh tat; outbox gia lap cung tat, khong gui Gmail/SMS that.
- Khi co nha cung cap email business va SMS, cau hinh HTTPS webhook, bat AccountMessages:EnableEmailDelivery, EnablePhoneDelivery, RequireVerifiedContacts va frontend flag tuong ung.
- Chua co dang nhap GitHub/OAuth.
- Co endpoint forgot/reset bang Identity token; forgot mail chi hoat dong khi email delivery duoc bat. Doi mat khau da dang nhap luon dung duoc.

6. Bai viet
- Entity Article da co cac trang thai:
  - Draft
  - Published
  - Archived
- Tao bai viet draft:
  - POST /api/v1/articles
- Cap nhat bai viet:
  - PUT /api/v1/articles/{id}
- Xuat ban bai viet:
  - POST /api/v1/articles/{id}/publish
- Luu tru/an bai viet:
  - POST /api/v1/articles/{id}/archive
- Danh sach bai viet public:
  - GET /api/v1/articles
- Xem bai viet public theo slug:
  - GET /api/v1/articles/{slug}
- Danh sach bai viet cua minh:
  - GET /api/v1/me/articles
- Chi tiet bai viet cua minh:
  - GET /api/v1/me/articles/{id}
- Bai moi tao la Draft.
- Draft chi tac gia xem duoc qua endpoint /me/articles.
- Public API chi hien bai Published.
- Archived an khoi public feed.
- Khong co xoa cung bai viet.
- Co optimistic concurrency bang Version.
- Version cu tra loi 409.
- Article body luu Markdown, chua render HTML.
- Co Article Series/collection: chu bai Published co the tao series, them/xoa bai va sap xep thu tu; series public theo slug.
- Metadata bai viet giai doan 1:
  - GET /api/v1/articles/{slug}/metadata
  - POST /api/v1/articles/{slug}/views
  - View count.
  - Like count.
  - Comment count.
  - Bookmark count.
  - Word count.
  - Reading time du kien.
  - PublishedAt/UpdatedAt.
- Article Revision giai doan 1:
  - Tu dong tao snapshot khi tao bai, sua bai, publish va archive.
  - GET /api/v1/me/articles/{id}/revisions
  - GET /api/v1/me/articles/{id}/revisions/{revisionId}
  - Luu title, slug, summary, body markdown, category, tag, status, version, editor va thoi gian.
  - Chua co compare diff va restore revision.
  - Autosave ban nhap qua PUT /api/v1/me/articles/{id}/autosave, chi danh cho tac gia va bai Draft.
  - Autosave kiem tra optimistic concurrency va khong tao revision moi moi lan luu.

11. Error handling
- Dung application/problem+json.
- Loi co traceId.
- Loi nghiep vu thuong co code.
- Mapping chinh:
  - 400: input/CSRF/domain validation.
  - 401: chua dang nhap hoac login sai.
  - 403: thieu quyen.
  - 404: khong tim thay/khong duoc doc.
  - 409: trung unique, stale version, dang duoc tham chieu.
  - 429: bi rate limit.
- PostgreSQL unique/foreign key/check violation duoc map sang response phu hop.
